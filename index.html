What the event is about

Five ways into the fort

Today you book each activity separately. FORT4SEASONS connects them: a visitor can join a history tour, rent a bike and finish with a tasting in a single booking, while each local partner stays independent.

How FortePass works
  1. Scan the QR code at the gateFortePass is a bilingual mobile website. There's nothing to install.
  2. Build your seasonal itineraryFilter by season, interests and the time you have. Book tours, bikes, events and tables together.
  3. Collect passport stampsCultural and environmental activities earn stamps. Stamps unlock discounts and extra content, and rewards follow participation rather than spending.
Who coordinates it. The proposed keystone organisation is Fondazione Forte Marghera, working with public authorities and partners, subject to agreement. Decisions on animal care stay with the animal-welfare team.

    Care · Kitten nursery

    Help orphaned kittens find a safe start

    We propose supporting a dedicated nursery for orphaned and vulnerable kittens at the fort, run with competent animal-welfare organisations. It does not exist yet. Before it opens, partners will check local need, premises, staff, veterinary supervision and budget.

    • A separate fundFortePass keeps donations apart from operating revenue and publishes documented transfers.
    • Professionals decideThe animal-welfare team decides on care, capacity and who may enter which areas.
    • Education, not entertainmentWorkshops happen in a separate space. Visitors don't handle kittens or feed the cats.
    • No rankings or prizesDonations and adoptions are never tied to leaderboards, prizes or competitions.
